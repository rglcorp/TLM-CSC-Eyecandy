The following changes were made to the character model of TouhouLittleMaid, licensed under CC-BY-NC-SA:

- Conversion from entity JSON model to OBJ model via Blockbench, for use with MTR Eyecandy
- The `hurtBlink` group of all characters has been removed, only displaying the active eyes
- The wand held by Flandre Scarlet has been tilted accordingly
- The sword held by Tenshi Hinanawi has been tilted accordingly
- The position of Wriggle Nightbug's Antenna has been adjusted
- The nuclear symbol of Utsuho Reiuji has been removed
- All accessories floating decoration from all characters has been removed (Except for Patchouli Knowledge, where the books are kept in-tact)
